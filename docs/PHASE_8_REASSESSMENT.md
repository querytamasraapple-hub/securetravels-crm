## Why a reassessment was the next step

Phase 8 is conditional in two places, not one:

- `ROADMAP.md` — "only if post-sale support volume justifies a dedicated module -
  **reassess at this point whether it's actually needed**".
- `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` — "Phase 8 - Service/Support Module *(reassess need
  here)*".

The instruction to reassess is the instruction. Building the module first and
assessing it afterwards would have inverted the order the roadmap asked for, and
would have produced a ticket system whose need was never established.

## Finding 1 — is there post-sale support volume? NOT demonstrable

**Question:** Is there evidence of customer-service volume — tickets, complaints,
support contacts — that a dedicated module would serve?

**Evidence gathered:**

- **No support module, complaint module or knowledge base exists anywhere in the
  repository.** No `Ticket`, `Complaint` or knowledge-base entity, no controller,
  no service, no migration. There is no system from which volume could be
  measured, which means there is no dashboard showing a backlog, no response-time
  history, and no record of how many support contacts arrive per week.
- **The only "complaint" in the codebase is a different word entirely.**
  `ConsentSuppression`, `EmailWebhookService` and `EmailMessage` handle an email
  provider's spam complaint — a hard bounce or spam report — which is a
  *consent and deliverability* concern, not a customer complaining about a trip.
  Conflating the two would be the easiest way to justify Phase 8 with evidence
  that does not support it.
- **`Task` is not a ticket and does not pretend to be.** Its own javadoc scopes it
  to automation: first contact, the +1/+3/+8/+15 cadence, quotation and payment
  reminders, ops handoffs. It is pre-sale lifecycle automation with SLA
  escalation, generated by rules rather than opened by a customer. Extending it
  into a support desk would mean a schema and status model for a different job.
- **No business evidence of post-sale support load.** No support volume figure, no
  complaints log, no escalation record, no "N tickets per week" statement exists
  in `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `ROADMAP.md` or any other document. Phase 7
  built accounts, opportunities, pipeline, commissions and reporting; none of
  them surfaced a post-sale workload.

**Decision:** the service/support module is **deferred**. Not rejected — there is
no evidence *against* it either, only an absence of evidence *for* it, and this
document does not claim otherwise.

**Next-reassessment trigger.** Re-run this gate immediately, before writing any
code, if **any** of these becomes real rather than aspirational:

1. A support or complaints request arrives through a channel no current feature
   handles, and is being tracked somewhere other than this system (email inbox,
   spreadsheet, WhatsApp group, someone's memory).
2. More than one person is handling post-sale customer contact as a substantial
   part of their week.
3. A customer-facing complaint channel (form, email address, phone number) is
   published.
4. A single missed or mishandled post-sale issue causes a real, named business
   loss — a refund, a bad review, a lost repeat customer.

If none of these is true, re-run this gate no earlier than the **Phase 9
(Finance Depth)** checkpoint.
